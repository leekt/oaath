Owner-phone delegation demo

Approve a bounded job on your phone, then run it from this page. The owner key never leaves the phone.

Local Anvil devnet

  1. 1

    Pair your phone

    Shows a one-time QR code. Scan it with the OAAth iOS app on the same network, then tap Pair in the app.

    Scan with your iPhone camera, then confirm in the app.
  2. 2

    Connect the account

    Loads the phone-owned account for this workspace.

  3. 3

    Request permission

    Asks your phone for 30 minutes of authority: one call selector, at most 5 wei per call, up to three jobs on each configured chain. Approve it on the phone.

  4. 4

    Run a job

    Sends one bounded call on the chosen chain using the approved permission.

  5. 5

    Check the job

    Looks up the saved job and waits for its onchain outcome. Reload the page first to see it recover; checking never sends the job again.

  6. 6

    Revoke the permission

    Stops new jobs and asks your phone to remove the permission on each chain. Approve each request on the phone, then check again until it is revoked everywhere.
