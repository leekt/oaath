# Automation creator
Mode: Operate. React + shadcn/ui, directly in code, selected by the user. A supplied creator for customers defining recurring purchases; DCA is the first recipe.

## Direction contract
THESIS: An account statement that starts as an instruction and becomes retained activity. The customer can see exactly what they permit before a wallet opens.
OWN-WORLD: Daylight use on a laptop or phone. Slate ink on cool white; a restrained deep blue action color. Sans-serif labels, tabular financial figures, thin row rules, spacious unboxed regions. Addresses sit behind disclosures.
STORY: Specify a purchase, review a frozen statement, authorize, then read confirmed history. No YAML knowledge needed.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A small product header above a large task title. Two-thirds left is a short purchase form; one-third right is a live exact-spend statement. The primary review action closes the form. On mobile the statement follows the inputs. The signature interaction replaces the editable statement with its immutable owner review in place, with a short opacity transition and reduced-motion support.
FORM: Account statement, candidate 7, seed 8a3b6375. Seven candidates: transaction ledger, calendar agenda, standing-order slip, exchange-board quote, workflow instruction sheet, recipe notebook, account statement. The seed's rail, cassette, catalog, botanical, Metro, and grid worlds lose product clarity; their rigor contributes aligned amounts, fixed limits, readable density, consistent state placement, clear type hierarchy, and responsive structure without adopting their motifs.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
