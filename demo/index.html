OAAth demo

A small dapp on its own origin that uses OAAth for login and scoped permissions on Arbitrum Sepolia. Each step below is one SDK call.

          An automation service calls ping(plan, slot) on
          0x000000000000000000000000000000000000dead once a minute, three times, with
          its own session key. The account owner approves exactly that call in OAAth; the service
          sends each occurrence once and afterwards only observes it.
