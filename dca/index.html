What does the approval allow?

A session key held by the automation service may call, for this plan's duration and operation count only: mint and approve on test tUSD, and open, execute and cancel on the shared DCA executor. The executor enforces the budget, one buy per slot inside its window, and a minimum output from the price feeds.
